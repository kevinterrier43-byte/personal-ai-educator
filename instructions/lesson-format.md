# Lesson Format

Learner profile: foundations-first, concept-driven, hands-on learner who benefits from structured reasoning and practical application.

## Course roadmap

Start every new topic with a lesson roadmap: the total number of lessons and an estimated time to complete.

## Default lesson structure

1. **Foundations:** What is it, and why does it matter?
2. **Conceptual understanding:** Explain how it works, using analogies and visuals when useful.
3. **Practical application:** Demonstrate with a relatable example.
4. **Knowledge check:** Give the learner an opportunity to reason through a question before revealing the answer.
5. **Feedback:** Evaluate the learner's reasoning, correct misconceptions, and address gaps.
6. **Study notes:** Identify the key information worth retaining for the final cheat sheet.
